## Career Radar v1.2.0 — Public Launch

Career Radar is evidence-based European tech-job intelligence for non-EU candidates who need visa sponsorship or relocation support.

This release is the first public-launch baseline built from the post-v1.1.4 hardening and repository cleanup work.

### Highlights

- **European technical-market scope** — the published catalog now excludes US-only and other out-of-scope vacancies at the data layer rather than hiding them only in the UI.
- **Evidence-first eligibility** — `eligible`, `unknown`, and `rejected` remain deterministic and inspectable; explicit work-authorization restrictions override weaker company-level sponsorship signals.
- **More reliable catalog delivery** — streamed downloads, size/SHA-256/gzip/schema validation, retries for transient transport failures, atomic promotion, and fail-closed cache preservation.
- **Faster desktop synchronization** — catalog reconciliation avoids the previous per-row lookup pathology and handles the production catalog within the hosted launch gate.
- **Real user-flow validation** — onboarding, recommendations, jobs, evidence, Apply URLs, saved/applied state, persistence, Companies, Coverage, Persian RTL, and Windows runtime flows were exercised against real catalog data.
- **Continuous market publication** — the durable `market-data` catalog is versioned and refreshed through the repository's ingestion workflow.
- **English and Persian UI** — including RTL support and localized deterministic recommendation explanations.
- **No paid LLM/API runtime dependency** — matching and eligibility remain deterministic.

### Windows — easiest way to run

Download **CareerRadar-Setup-v1.2.0.exe** and launch Career Radar after installation. A portable ZIP is also provided.

Python, Node.js, Docker, PostgreSQL, and project dependencies are bundled for the desktop package and are not required on the user's machine.

Verify the downloaded installer or portable archive with the hashes in **SHA256SUMS.txt** before running it.

### Data and eligibility notes

Career Radar intentionally prefers false negatives over false-positive sponsorship claims. A sponsor-register entry alone does not mean that every vacancy at that company offers sponsorship. Missing or ambiguous evidence remains `unknown`, and hard restrictions such as no sponsorship or existing-work-authorization requirements result in `rejected`.

Employer policies, vacancies, and immigration rules can change after collection. Career Radar is job intelligence, not legal or immigration advice, and does not guarantee sponsorship for any applicant.
