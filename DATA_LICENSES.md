employer pages, government registers, dependencies, or other upstream material.

## Job-board data

Job records are factual extracts from public employer ATS endpoints in the verified
source registry. Job-description rights remain with the relevant employer or
publisher. Users and redistributors must follow upstream terms, robots policies,
retention requirements, and applicable law. Career Radar preserves source and apply
URLs for provenance.

## Sponsor registers and catalog snapshots

`data/sponsors.csv.gz` is a normalized evidence index derived from official public
registers. The upstream authority remains authoritative. Registry presence is evidence,
not legal advice or a guarantee that a vacancy or candidate will be sponsored.

Generated catalogs combine project-owned schemas and transformations with third-party
factual and employer-authored material. Redistributors should preserve provenance,
generation timestamps, eligibility evidence, required notices, and this document; they
must not present stale snapshots as current vacancies.

No candidate profiles, notes, application records, API keys, or access tokens belong in
published catalog snapshots.
